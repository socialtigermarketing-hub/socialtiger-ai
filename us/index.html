Our Story

The Social Tiger was built from challenges we experienced ourselves and saw repeatedly in other businesses. We spent years balancing client work, sales, marketing, follow-up, and everything else needed to keep a business moving forward. There was always something demanding attention, and growth often felt like something we would get around to once everything else was handled.


Great companies were delivering incredible results for their clients, yet they struggled to get enough visibility. Marketing often took a back seat to more urgent priorities, while websites and other tools were not contributing as much as they could. At the same time, valuable hours were being lost to repetitive tasks, disconnected systems, and processes that made growth harder than it needed to be.


Owners were working hard and their teams were doing their best, yet many still felt stuck. Trying to grow while managing everything happening behind the scenes often felt overwhelming. The solution often came down to visibility in front of the right leads, consistency with a strategy that fit the business, and having the right systems in place.


We became passionate about helping businesses solve these challenges without adding more complexity. Businesses need practical solutions that help them get noticed, earn trust, and create more time to focus on serving their customers. That belief became The Social Tiger.

Common Questions

How can B2B service businesses automate lead qualification?

Automating lead qualification requires a multimodal discovery pipeline. Instead of relying purely on cold outreach, businesses should implement a zero-click optimization matrix—using high-value content, optimized profiles, and conversational AI systems to pre-qualify prospects before they ever book a call. This ensures your sales team only speaks with leads who already understand your value and meet your criteria.

What is a CRM pipeline synthesis system?

A CRM pipeline synthesis system is an integrated approach that connects your visibility directly to your outbound pipeline. Unlike traditional marketing, it is designed to capture interest, nurture trust, and guide prospects seamlessly into booking a consultation or requesting a proposal.
