This guide provides a checklist to optimise your LinkedIn profile, helping you achieve business objectives and scale your conversion rate efficiently.

There are more than 30 areas of your LinkedIn profile you could edit, adapt, and optimise. We have selected the top 6 that matter most for conversions. Work through each section and ask yourself the questions. If your answer is "no," you know exactly what to improve.

1. Profile Photo (1000 x 1000 pixels)

Does your expression match how you want to be perceived?

Your expression should convey warmth, professionalism, or authority depending on the impression you want to make. A friendly smile makes you approachable, while a serious expression communicates expertise and confidence.

Does your background complement your face?

Keep it simple and neutral. Ensure your face stands out and is the focal point.

Is your photo consistent across platforms?

Use the same or a similar image across social media, email signature, and marketing materials to maintain brand recognition.

Is the photo high-resolution and clear?

It should be sharp, crisp, and at least 1000 x 1000 pixels to appear professional on all devices.

2. Banner Photo (1584 x 396 pixels)

Does it state who you help and how?

Your banner should clearly communicate your target audience and your value proposition in a concise sentence.

Does it highlight the outcomes of your service?

Show the transformation clients can expect, such as time saved, increased revenue, or business growth. Focus on results rather than features.

Does it include authority-building elements?

Incorporate client logos, industry awards, media mentions, or measurable results that demonstrate expertise.

Is the design professional?

Ensure it is clean, visually consistent with your brand, and legible on all devices.

Is the photo high-resolution and clear?

It should be sharp, crisp, and at least 1584 x 396 pixels to appear professional on LinkedIn.

3. Headline

Does it highlight the transformation you provide?

Clearly describe the results or outcomes your clients experience. Avoid listing your job title alone.

Does it call out your target audience?

Be specific about the roles, industries, or types of companies you help. This immediately signals relevance.

Does it include authority builders?

Include measurable proof or achievements that demonstrate credibility. For example, "Helped 50+ UK service businesses increase inbound enquiries through LinkedIn."

Are the first words highly impactful?

The opening 7–10 words are most visible. Use action-oriented language to communicate value immediately.

4. Featured Section (1200 x 627 pixels for images)

Have you pinned high-value assets?

Include resources that support your business goals, such as free guides, service pages, or helpful content for potential clients.

Do the thumbnails create urgency?

Use visuals and text that convey importance or immediate value. Keep them clear and attention-grabbing.

Are the thumbnails mobile-friendly?

Maintain simple, uncluttered visuals. Stick to 1200 x 627 pixels for optimal display.

Do you include clear CTAs?

Each featured item should prompt a next step, like downloading a resource, visiting a page, or booking a call. Frame the CTA as a tangible benefit.

5. About Section

Does it start with a hook?

Open with a line that grabs attention by addressing a key pain point your audience faces.

Have you emphasised the problem?

Describe the impact of not addressing the issue, including financial, time, or growth consequences.

Do you outline the transformation you provide?

Be specific about the results clients can expect. Focus on outcomes rather than processes.

Have you included proof?

Use metrics, testimonials, or before-and-after results to build trust. Include real numbers and client outcomes when possible.

Is there a clear and simple CTA?

End with a single next step, such as contacting you for a consultation or exploring your services.

6. Experience Section

Have you shared outcome-led stories?

Highlight the challenges you solved and the results achieved. Each role should demonstrate your expertise and the value you provide.

Have you emphasised results?

Quantify your impact, for example, "Increased client enquiries by 40% in six months." Include links or visuals as proof.

Is your writing client-focused?

Write to educate and support potential clients rather than just describing yourself. Show how your role helps them achieve goals.

Do you include CTAs?

End each experience entry with a low-friction next step, like booking a consultation or learning more about your services.
